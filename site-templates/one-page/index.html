What I do

Three things, not seven. If everything is important then nothing is.

The first thing

A couple of sentences. Say what someone gets, not what you use to make it.

The second thing

Same again. Keep them roughly the same length — it looks deliberate.

The third thing

If you only have two, delete this one. Three is not a rule.

Who I am

A short paragraph in your own voice. This is the part people actually read, and the part that is hardest to fake, so it is worth more than the rest of the page put together.

Say where you are. Say what you did before. One human detail beats three credentials.

Get in touch

Say exactly how, and how quickly you answer. A form nobody trusts is worse than an email address.
